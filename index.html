About

A. Music Together lets you listen with a friend who is hosting a session in A. Music on Android. You hear the same song at the same moment, on any phone, tablet or computer.

How to use it

  • Your friend opens Listening together in A. Music and starts a session.
  • Type the code from their screen here and tap Join.
  • On iPhone, tap Tap for sound once: Safari only plays sound after a tap.
  • Open Up next (bottom right) to see what's coming, move or remove songs, or paste a link: YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal and more. Links from other services are found on YouTube Music by your friend's app.
  • Tap ♥ or ✕ to tell the host what you think of a song.

Install it

  • iPhone / iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android, Windows, Mac: the install icon in the address bar, or the browser menu → Install app.

Private by design

No account and nothing to sign up for. The session code is the key: it names a private channel and locks every message (AES-256). Only tiny messages pass between you (song, play or pause, position), through two public relays.

Good to know

  • The host controls play, pause and skipping. You can add, move and remove songs in Up next.
  • On iPhone the sound stops when the screen locks.
  • Songs come from YouTube and SoundCloud. A few YouTube songs can't play outside YouTube; Bandcamp songs play on the host's phone only.
